4. End Words (own word, last, with a space — 24 K UB · 27W L TO · 12 KI THOR)

UBUnbalancedCalls the opposite X over. He stays on the line and lands far — a wide split, or a unit outside the widest man already there. Needs an even surface to take the receiver from.
TOTwo Tight EndsUB's twin. Needs one odd surface (the TE being doubled) and one even (the WR who crosses). He arrives as a second Y on the line, so that side reads G T Y Y; the side he left is bare. Receivers there slide out with the line — the wing flanks outside both Y's.
THORTackles RightBoth tackles to the right — the line becomes G C G T T. Eligibles on that side slide out past the new end man; the backside tight end closes down to the guard. A split receiver stays split.
LOKITackles LeftThe same, both tackles to the left. Thor and Loki are absolute — they do not follow strength.

5. Key Points

✓  Backs = 5 − total eligibles. 2 elig = full house, 5 = empty, 6 = illegal.
✓  Surface numbers say how many eligibles are on that side — nothing else.
✓  Modifiers change alignment only. They never change the count.
✓  Read the call left side, right side, then backfield tag, then any end words.
✓  Modifiers ride the number (24B). End words stand alone (24 K UB).
✓  A tag whose back count doesn't match the formation is ignored with a warning.
✓  King and Queen are mirrors of each other — so are SG/WG, KI/QI, K2G/Q2G.
Examples: 24B I · 44W L · 18P D · QP2 · 21 K2G · 24 K UB · 27W L TO · 12 QI THOR
